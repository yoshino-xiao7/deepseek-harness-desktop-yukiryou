## 简体中文

`v1.0.11` 修复侧栏更新按钮与余额插件同时出现时，设置行被撑高、按钮错位的问题。

- 余额插件保留完整行，设置与更新按钮在下一行保持对齐。
- 更新按钮入场动画只为自身预留空间，避免挤压其他侧栏插件。
- 覆盖窄侧栏、折叠侧栏、下载百分比和不确定进度，以及更新完成、手动更新与更新失败状态。

内置 DeepSeek Harness 继续使用 `0.1.5-rc.2`。

---

## English

`v1.0.11` fixes the stretched Settings row and misaligned update button when the sidebar balance plugin and update action appear together.

- Keeps the balance plugin on a full-width row, with Settings and the update button aligned on the following row.
- Reserves space only for the update button during its entrance animation, without squeezing other sidebar plugins.
- Covers narrow and collapsed sidebars, percentage and indeterminate download progress, and downloaded, manual-update, and update-error states.

The bundled DeepSeek Harness remains at `0.1.5-rc.2`.
